# Get Help

This page lists where to go for help with UMH Core, and which contact fits which request. UMH keeps four routes separate, so that a request reaches the people who handle it: the community on Discord, confidential security reports, contracted support, and commercial requests such as renewals and licensing.

## Community questions

Questions about installing, configuring and using UMH Core go to the UMH community on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/F9mqkZnm9d).

## Security vulnerabilities

Report security vulnerabilities to `security@umh.app`. The address accepts vulnerability reports only, so keep ordinary problems on the other routes. [Our security policy](https://github.com/united-manufacturing-hub/united-manufacturing-hub/blob/staging/SECURITY.md) describes what happens after you send a report, and how a fix is announced.

## Enterprise and contracted support

For enterprise or separately contracted support, follow the support process in the applicable agreement. The standard SLA lists `support@umh.app`; individually agreed arrangements take precedence. Coverage and response times depend on your contract. The standard terms are published at [terms.umh.app](https://terms.umh.app/).

## Commercial and account requests

Renewals, licensing and paid add-ons go through your account executive. If you do not have one, use [the contact form](https://www.umh.app/talk-to-team) to reach the UMH team about an Enterprise license or any other commercial request.
