# Get Help

This page lists where to go for help with UMH Core, and which contact fits which request. UMH keeps three routes separate, so that a request reaches the people who handle it: technical support, confidential security reports, and commercial requests such as renewals and licensing.

## Technical support

**Enterprise:** Email **support@umh.app**. This is the support channel covered by your agreement and applicable SLAs. You’re welcome to contact your Value Engineer directly, but SLA coverage does not apply to direct messages. If your agreement defines a different support process, that process takes precedence. Coverage and response times depend on your contract. The standard terms are published at [terms.umh.app](https://terms.umh.app/).

**Community:** Ask installation, configuration, and usage questions in the UMH community on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/F9mqkZnm9d).

## Security vulnerabilities

Report security vulnerabilities to `security@umh.app`. The address accepts vulnerability reports only, so keep ordinary problems on the other routes. [Our security policy](https://github.com/united-manufacturing-hub/united-manufacturing-hub/blob/staging/SECURITY.md) describes what happens after you send a report, and how a fix is announced.

## Commercial and account requests

Renewals, licensing and paid add-ons go through your account executive. If you do not have one, use [the contact form](https://www.umh.app/talk-to-team) to reach the UMH team about an Enterprise license or any other commercial request.
