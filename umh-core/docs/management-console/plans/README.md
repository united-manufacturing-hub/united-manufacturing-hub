# Licenses & Plans

By default, every account in the Management Console is assigned a Community License. The licence is stored on your company, not on individual users or instances, and it applies to everyone who signs in to that company.

The console shows the current state in three places:

- The sidebar shows **Enterprise Edition** or **Community Edition** under the Management Console name.
- The **Enterprise License Status** card on the home page shows **Active** with the expiry date, or **Inactive**. The date turns red when fewer than three months remain.
- **Settings → Account → License** shows the same as the card on the home page.

## What a licence controls

The licence decides which edition your company is on. Without an active licence you are on the Community Edition, with one you are on the Enterprise Edition. The differences between the two, including the one-instance limit on Community, are listed in the [Editions Reference](editions-reference.md).

## Applying a licence

Activating the Enterprise plan is a fully managed, white-glove process. There are no licence keys to enter, no files to upload and no configuration to change on your side. Once your agreement is in place, your dedicated account executive provisions the Enterprise licence for your company centrally, and the entitlement is applied to your entire organization at once: every user, every instance, every site.

The change takes effect the next time you open the console. The sidebar switches to **Enterprise Edition**, the **Enterprise License Status** card on the home page shows **Active** with your contract end date, and all Enterprise capabilities are available to your team immediately, with no downtime and no action required from your users.

Should the console still show Community Edition after activation, sign out and back in so it picks up the new entitlement. If that does not resolve it, your account executive is your single point of contact and will take it from there.

To get a licence, or to check what it costs, see https://www.umh.app/pricing or contact your account executive.

## When a licence expires

The console checks the expiry date every time it loads. On the first page load after the date has passed:

- The sidebar shows **Community Edition** and the home page card shows **Inactive**.
- Registering new instances is blocked once the company has one or more instances. Existing instances keep running and stay connected; nothing is deleted.
- Existing users keep their accounts and roles. You cannot invite new users or change roles until the licence is renewed.
- The Enterprise-only controls listed in the [Editions Reference](editions-reference.md) are hidden or disabled.

The console does not send a reminder before expiry. Watch the date on the home page card and contact your account executive to renew.
